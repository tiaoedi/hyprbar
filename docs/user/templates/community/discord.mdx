This release does not ship a Discord-specific template or verify Discord-client compatibility. The screenshot and client list inherited from upstream documentation have been removed because they showed upstream Noctalia theme names and a third-party account.

You may find third-party Discord themes in the community-template catalog. They are not maintained or tested by Hyprbar; inspect their source and verify compatibility with your client before enabling them. The catalog endpoint is inherited from upstream. `[shell].offline_mode = true` blocks new catalog/template HTTP requests made through Hyprbar's shared client; content already cached locally may remain readable. This setting does not control browser, other-process, or arbitrary plugin network activity.

## Setup

1. Enable community templates only if you trust the upstream catalog and the selected template.
2. Follow the documentation for your Discord client and the particular theme you choose.

## Troubleshooting

- **Theme not appearing:** Confirm that the third-party theme supports your client and is installed using that client's current instructions.
- **Colors not updating:** Check the theme's own requirements; this fork does not guarantee Discord integration.

## Additional Notes

Do not assume that a community theme automatically follows Hyprbar colors unless its source explicitly implements that integration.

Freedo artwork notice
=====================

Asset: `freedo.svg` and the Freedo image used in `hyprbar-wallpaper.svg`.

Source: GNU Project, `https://www.gnu.org/graphics/Freedo_improved.svg`.

credits the cleaned-up version to Rubén Rodríguez Pérez and the current SVG
changes (nostrils, open eyes, and a new brush) to Islene Calciolari Garcia and
Alexandre Oliva. A former character version called Freetz was drawn by
Burnaron; that is historical context, not authorship of this SVG. GNU lists
